# Insights and Recommendations

## Key Insights

These insights are based only on values visible in the repository files.

### Revenue Performance

The customer ranking extract shows analysed revenue of GBP 17.74m. Monthly KPI exports show clear seasonality, with stronger trading in the final quarter and a visible peak in November 2011 at GBP 1.51m. The lowest exported monthly revenue is February 2011 at GBP 0.52m.

Business meaning:

- Trading performance is not evenly distributed across the year.
- Commercial reporting should compare performance by month and season rather than relying only on annual totals.

### Product Concentration

The product Pareto export shows that approximately 1,037 out of 4,914 products, or 21.1%, contribute around 80% of product revenue.

The top 10 products contribute 8.0% of product revenue in the product Pareto export.

Business meaning:

- A relatively small group of products drives most revenue.
- Availability, pricing, and promotional execution for these products are commercially important.

### Customer Concentration

The customer ranking export shows that approximately 1,352 out of 5,878 known customers, or 23.0%, contribute around 80% of known-customer revenue.

Business meaning:

- Revenue is concentrated among a relatively small group of customers.
- Retention and service quality for high-value customers are important to protect revenue.

### Repeat Behaviour

The exported repeat-rate view shows an average monthly repeat rate of 23.8%.

Business meaning:

- Repeat purchasing is measurable and should be tracked as a retention indicator.
- The business may benefit from customer lifecycle analysis, including new, retained, and reactivated customer groups.

### Returns

The return-rate export shows that return revenue and return invoice rates vary by month.

The highest return rate by amount in the exported view is December 2011 at 32.1%.

Business meaning:

- Gross sales alone may overstate commercial performance.
- Return monitoring should be part of monthly trading review.

## Business Recommendations

- Protect stock availability and merchandising focus for top revenue-driving products.
- Review low-contribution products to identify range rationalisation, bundling, or promotional opportunities.
- Create a high-value customer monitoring view to support retention activity.
- Analyse return rates by product and month to identify quality, fulfilment, or product description issues.
- Track B2B-style high-value invoice activity separately from normal retail orders so that average order value and monthly trends are not distorted. In the current invoice segment export, B2B-segment invoices contribute 1.7% of invoice revenue.
- Add customer cohort analysis to understand whether revenue growth comes from new customers, repeat customers, or reactivated customers.
