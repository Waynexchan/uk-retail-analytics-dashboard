# Business Problem

## Context

This project analyses UK online retail transaction data for a commercial reporting use case. The business needs a clear view of sales performance, customer behaviour, product concentration, and returns so that trading decisions can be based on evidence rather than raw transaction extracts.

## Business Objective

Build a Power BI dashboard supported by SQL-transformed data that allows a commercial or retail analytics team to:

- Monitor monthly revenue, orders, customers, units, and average order value.
- Understand which products and customers contribute most to revenue.
- Track repeat purchase behaviour.
- Monitor returns as a potential pressure on net revenue.
- Separate unusually high-value invoice behaviour from standard retail activity.

## Stakeholders

- Commercial analyst or trading analyst reviewing monthly performance.
- Merchandising team reviewing product performance and range concentration.
- Customer or CRM team reviewing repeat behaviour and high-value customers.
- Business leadership needing an executive summary of performance.

## Current Portfolio Gap Addressed

The original repository showed the technical dashboard work but did not fully explain the business problem, stakeholder need, or commercial decision context. This document frames the analysis as a business case study suitable for UK junior data analyst and commercial analyst applications.
